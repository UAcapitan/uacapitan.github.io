A few helpful answers

Where can I download your games?+

Our published Android games are available on Google Play. Choose a game above to open its official listing. Availability can vary by device and region.

Are the games free to play?+

The games in our published collection are free to download. They may include advertising or optional in-app purchases. Check the individual Google Play listing for details.

Can I play without an internet connection?+

Several titles support offline gameplay, including My Mines, Cubic Snake, Legendary Ninja, and Block Boom Saga. Ads, downloads, purchases, and some online features require an internet connection. See each game’s store listing for details.

How do you handle my data?+

Our privacy policy explains game analytics, advertising services, local saves, and your choices. This website does not add analytics trackers or advertising cookies.
